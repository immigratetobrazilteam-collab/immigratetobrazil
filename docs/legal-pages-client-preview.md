# Legal Pages Client-Side Preview

Last updated: March 23, 2026

This document shows what the legal family should look like on the client side before implementation. It is written as a front-end preview, not as an engineering plan. Each page below includes the visible hero content, the section sequence, the publish-ready body copy direction, the related internal links, and the official public resources that should appear in the sidebar or resource block.

The tone combines two requirements:

- premium, structured, procedural, confidence-building
- still readable by immigrants, families, founders, and professionals who need direct answers

The rule for the whole family is simple: each page must sound authoritative without sounding generic, and clear without sounding casual.

#### Section 1. Purpose of this policy

**Lead:** `This policy defines our privacy framework in operational terms.`

This Privacy Policy defines how Immigrate to Brazil handles personal information collected through our website, our consultation and intake forms, our direct communication channels, and our service-delivery workflow. It explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, when we may share it, how long we retain it, and how a person can request access, correction, deletion, or clarification.

The purpose of this policy is not only disclosure. It is control. Immigration matters often involve identity records, chronology, government notices, family documents, translations, and payment confirmations. Those records require a privacy framework that supports lawful handling, operational discipline, and controlled access.

#### Section 2. Scope of data collected

**Strap:** `Personal data is collected according to function, not curiosity.`

We may collect client-identification data such as name, nationality, contact details, country of residence, occupation, and other information needed to establish who is contacting us and how the matter should be handled at intake stage.

Where a person submits records or seeks advisory or execution support, we may also process immigration-related documentation and factual records, including status history, travel chronology, authority notices, family-relationship records, procedural background, filings, translations, and payment confirmations relevant to the matter.

We may additionally process technical and usage data connected to the operation of the platform, including browser data, access logs, interaction patterns, cookie-related settings, accessibility settings, and platform-performance data where lawfully used.

#### Section 3. Data collection channels

**Strap:** `The collection channel affects the purpose and the level of control.`

Data may be collected through:

- consultation and intake forms
- direct contact by email
- phone and WhatsApp communication
- payment-related correspondence
- document submission
- website interactions and tracking systems

We do not treat all channels as interchangeable. The privacy implications of a form submission, a payment proof email, and a WhatsApp urgency message are not identical. For that reason, the collection channel is part of the governance framework.

#### Section 4. Purpose of processing

**Strap:** `Personal data is processed to support advisory work, execution control, and lawful operations.`

We process personal information to assess route fit, review chronology, communicate with clients and prospective clients, manage consultation requests, verify payments, schedule appointments, coordinate execution support, maintain internal records, support compliance and audit logic, prevent fraud, manage disputes, and protect legal rights where necessary.

In practical terms, we do not process information because it happens to be available. We process it because immigration advisory work depends on record control, communication accuracy, and legally defensible handling of sensitive operational information.

#### Section 5. Legal basis for processing

**Strap:** `Legal basis follows function.`

Depending on context, our legal basis for processing may include contractual necessity, pre-contractual steps taken at the request of the data subject, legitimate interest, legal obligation, protection of rights in judicial or administrative settings, and consent where consent is the appropriate basis for the specific activity.

We do not rely on consent as a blanket explanation for all processing. When another legal basis more accurately reflects the function being performed, we use that basis. That is part of a serious compliance approach.

#### Section 6. Data sharing and third parties

**Strap:** `Sharing is controlled and purpose-based.`

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with professional collaborators, infrastructure providers, communication providers, translation providers, payment-support tools, legal professionals, or government authorities where that sharing is required for lawful operations, service execution, or legal obligation.

When a matter requires external involvement, only information relevant to that stage should be shared. In practical terms, the standard is necessity, not convenience.

#### Section 7. International data transfers

**Strap:** `Cross-border operations require explicit acknowledgement.`

Immigrate to Brazil serves an international client base. For that reason, personal data may be stored, accessed, reviewed, or transmitted across jurisdictions where this is necessary for communications, intake handling, infrastructure, document review, or client coordination.

International transfer does not reduce our duty of care. It means the platform operates in a cross-border environment while still applying a controlled privacy model centered on lawful purpose, proportionality, and access discipline.

#### Section 8. Data retention framework

**Strap:** `Retention follows legal, operational, and evidentiary need.`

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for ongoing service handling, for legal and compliance recordkeeping, for payment and dispute review, for fraud prevention, or for the defense of rights.

Retention is not indefinite by default. The appropriate period depends on the type of information, the stage of the matter, the existence of a payment record, the existence of an active or completed service relationship, and the legal or evidentiary risk associated with the record.

#### Section 9. Data subject rights

**Strap:** `Rights requests should be clear and specific.`

Subject to applicable law, data subjects may have rights including access, confirmation of processing, correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, deletion where legally available, restriction of certain processing, portability where applicable, information about sharing, and objection where recognized by law.

Requests should be sent to `immigratetobrazilteam@gmail.com`. We may ask for reasonable identity verification before acting on a request, especially where the request concerns sensitive records, service files, deletion, or disclosure.

#### Section 10. Security and data protection measures

**Strap:** `Privacy protection depends on process, not slogans.`

We use process-based safeguards to support secure handling of information. These may include controlled access, need-to-know review, communication discipline, structured intake handling, record organization, payment-verification controls, and secure storage or transfer practices appropriate to the tools in use.

No digital platform can promise absolute security. What we can say is that privacy protection is treated as an operational control issue inside our service model.

#### Section 11. Contact for data requests

Privacy, correction, deletion, and data-rights requests should be directed to `immigratetobrazilteam@gmail.com`.

### Main content

#### Section 1. Purpose of cookies in platform operation

Cookies and similar technologies are used as part of the technical and operational framework of the platform. Their role is not uniform and should not be treated as such.

Certain cookies are strictly necessary to enable the platform to function in a stable, secure, and accessible manner. These may support session continuity, navigation behavior, consent recording, accessibility configurations, and core technical processes explicitly requested by the user.

Other technologies may be used to observe how the platform performs in practice. This includes identifying structural friction points, navigation inefficiencies, and areas where usability or clarity can be improved.

Where additional technologies are used for optional features or integrations, their function must be clearly separated from essential operations. Each category of cookies serves a distinct purpose and is governed accordingly.

The platform does not treat all cookies as interchangeable. Their use is structured, purpose-bound, and proportionate to the function they support.

#### Section 2. Categories of cookies used

Cookies and similar technologies are organized into functional categories to ensure clarity, control, and appropriate governance.

The platform distinguishes between:

- essential cookies
- analytical cookies
- optional preference or marketing cookies
- third-party integration cookies

This categorization is not merely descriptive. It reflects differences in necessity, legal basis, and consent requirements.

Essential cookies are tied to core platform functionality and are treated differently from optional technologies. Analytical and optional cookies are subject to stricter control, particularly where user consent is required under applicable data protection frameworks.

#### Section 3. Essential cookies

Essential cookies are required for the proper operation of the platform and are directly linked to services or functionalities explicitly requested by the user.

These may include:

- session management and navigation continuity
- security-related controls and abuse prevention
- consent recording and preference storage
- accessibility-related configurations
- technical infrastructure required for page delivery

These cookies do not operate for profiling or marketing purposes. Their function is limited to ensuring that the platform behaves as expected in a secure and consistent manner.

Because of their operational necessity, essential cookies may be deployed without prior consent where permitted by applicable law. However, their use remains limited to what is strictly required for functionality.

Disabling essential cookies at the browser level may result in degraded performance, restricted access to certain features, or complete inability to use parts of the platform.

#### Section 4. Analytical cookies

Analytical cookies are used to understand how the platform performs in real-world conditions and how users interact with its structure.

This may include:

- page interaction patterns
- navigation flow between sections
- time spent on pages
- identification of technical issues or performance bottlenecks

The purpose of analytical processing is limited to improving usability, clarity, and system performance. It is not intended to create individual user profiles or to support behavioral targeting.

Where possible, analytical data is aggregated, anonymized, or otherwise minimized to reduce the impact on individual privacy.

The use of analytical cookies is subject to applicable legal requirements. Where consent is required, these cookies will only be activated after such consent has been obtained.

#### Section 5. Optional cookies and tracking systems

Optional cookies include technologies that are not required for the operation of the platform but may enhance functionality, personalization, or communication.

These may include:

- preference-based features
- marketing or remarketing technologies
- extended tracking tools beyond basic analytics

Optional means optional. The refusal of these technologies must not prevent access to core legal information, consultation pathways, or essential service functionality.

Such cookies will only be activated where a valid consent mechanism has been provided and accepted by the user.

The platform does not deploy optional tracking technologies by default. Activation occurs only after an affirmative user action where required by law.

#### Section 6. Third-party integrations

The platform may incorporate third-party tools or services that rely on their own cookies or similar technologies.

These may include:

- analytics providers
- embedded communication tools
- scheduling systems
- external content integrations

Where such tools are used, their function is evaluated in relation to the overall platform structure. Their presence does not remove the obligation to ensure transparency, proportionality, and proper consent handling.

Third-party providers may process data according to their own policies. However, their integration into the platform is subject to internal review and alignment with applicable data protection principles.

Users should be aware that interacting with third-party features may result in data being processed outside the direct control of the platform.

#### Section 7. User control mechanisms

Users retain control over the use of cookies through multiple mechanisms.

These include:

- browser-level settings allowing deletion, restriction, or blocking of cookies
- device-level privacy configurations
- any consent or preference management interface provided on the platform

Browser-level controls operate independently of the platform. If cookies are restricted or blocked at this level, certain functionalities may be affected.

This may include:

- loss of saved preferences
- reduced accessibility behavior
- interruptions in session continuity
- limited functionality in certain areas

Such outcomes should be understood as a direct result of user-configured restrictions rather than an error or malfunction of the platform.

Where consent mechanisms are implemented, users may update or withdraw their preferences at any time, subject to technical limitations.

#### Section 8. Relationship with the Privacy Policy

This Cookies Policy operates as a specific extension of the broader data governance framework described in the Privacy Policy.

The Privacy Policy addresses:

- categories of personal data processed
- legal bases for processing
- data sharing and international transfers
- user rights under applicable law

This page focuses exclusively on the role of cookies and similar technologies within that framework.

Both documents should be read together to obtain a complete understanding of how data is handled across the platform.

#### Section 9. Updates and version control

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect:

- changes in technology
- updates in legal or regulatory requirements
- modifications to platform structure or services

The most current version will always be available on this page. Continued use of the platform after updates may indicate acceptance of the revised terms, where applicable.

This emergency route does not promise instant availability, automatic case acceptance, guaranteed intervention, or guaranteed success. It is a triage mechanism, not a guarantee mechanism.
