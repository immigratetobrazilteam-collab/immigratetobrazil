- H2: When It May Not Be the Right Fit
- The ordinary naturalisation route is not always the strongest option.

- More residence planning before citizenship.

- Record correction or status review before filing.
- Consultation before choosing the citizenship route.
- Monique helps clients avoid choosing a route based only on the category name. She reviews whether the client's purpose, timing, and longer-term plans truly support the ordinary naturalisation route.
