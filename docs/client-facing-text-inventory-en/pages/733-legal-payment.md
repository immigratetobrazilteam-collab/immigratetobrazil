### Pricing Model
- Cinematic background of the meeting of the Rio Negro and Solimoes near Manaus in Brazil for the Pricing model section on the Payment Terms page.
- H2: Pricing model
- Monique Fernandes charges according to the service stage involved.
- Consultation, review, and broader legal work are not all the same product. The applicable fee depends on the stage of service, the scope being discussed, and what has actually been agreed.

### Accepted Payment Methods
- Cinematic background of the waterways and dunes of the Delta do Parnaiba in Brazil for the Accepted payment methods section on the Payment Terms page.
- Section 02
- H2: Accepted payment methods
- Only the payment methods confirmed by the practice should be used.
- Accepted methods may include the options published by the practice at the relevant time. Clients should use current payment instructions and should not rely on outdated details from old messages without confirmation.

### Billing Structure
- Cinematic background of the wide coastline and water of Sao Miguel dos Milagres in Brazil for the Billing structure section on the Payment Terms page.
- Section 03
- H2: Billing structure
- Billing should reflect the work stage, not create confusion about scope.
- Payment for a consultation usually covers that consultation stage. If broader work is later agreed, it should be treated as a separate or expanded service stage unless clearly stated otherwise.

### Scope Versus Payment
- Cinematic background of the architecture and streets of Campos do Jordao in Brazil for the Scope versus payment section on the Payment Terms page.
- Section 04
- H2: Scope versus payment
- A payment should be linked to a clearly understood service stage.
- Clients should not assume that payment for one step automatically includes later filings, document preparation, or ongoing representation unless that broader scope has been expressly confirmed.

### Verification Booking Control And Scheduling Discipline
- Cinematic background of Amazon river in Brazil for the Verification, booking control, and scheduling discipline section on the Payment Terms page.
- Section 05
- H2: Verification, booking control, and scheduling discipline
- Payment may need to be verified before time is treated as reserved.
- Monique may require proof of payment and manual confirmation before consultation or other scheduled work is treated as fully booked. This helps avoid confusion, duplication, and timing disputes.

### Security And Payment Integrity
- Cinematic background of the wide coastline and water of Praia dos Carneiros in Brazil for the Security and payment integrity section on the Payment Terms page.
- Section 06
- H2: Security and payment integrity
- Payment handling should reduce the risk of mistakes and fraud.
- Clients should verify payment instructions, keep proof of payment, and use official contact channels if something seems inconsistent. The practice may also review payment details before confirming the next step.

### Non Payment Consequences
- Cinematic background of the Palacio da Alvorada in Brasilia in Brazil for the Non-payment consequences section on the Payment Terms page.
- Section 07
- H2: Non-payment consequences
- Work and scheduling may pause if payment has not been completed or confirmed.
- If an applicable fee remains unpaid or unverified, Monique may delay booking, review, or the next service stage until the issue is resolved.

### Payment Assistance And Financial Considerations
- Cinematic background of the architecture and streets of Sanctuary of Congonhas in Brazil for the Payment assistance and financial considerations section on the Payment Terms page.
- Section 08
- H2: Payment assistance and financial considerations
- Questions should be raised early if payment timing or method is an issue.
- If a client needs clarification about payment method, confirmation, or timing, it is better to ask before sending funds or relying on a slot.

- Section 09
- H2: Relationship with legal contracts and governing terms
- Payment rules work alongside the broader service terms of the practice.
- These terms should be read together with the general terms, refund policy, and any specific written engagement terms that apply to the matter.
