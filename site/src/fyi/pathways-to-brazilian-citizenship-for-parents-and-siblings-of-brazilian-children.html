🇧🇷 Pathways to Brazilian Citizenship for Parents and Siblings of Brazilian Children 🇧🇷 For parents and siblings of a Brazilian child, Brazilian immigration law provides structured pathways to permanent residency and, potentially, citizenship. Parents of a minor Brazilian citizen may apply for permanent residency, which can lead to eligibility for naturalisation after just one year of residence in Brazil, provided legal requirements are met. Siblings, as family members connected by direct lineage, may also seek residency based on their relationship to the Brazilian citizen. In exceptional cases, this residency may likewise allow access to naturalisation after one year.
