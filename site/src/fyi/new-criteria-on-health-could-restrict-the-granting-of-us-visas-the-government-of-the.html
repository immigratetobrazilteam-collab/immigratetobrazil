New health criteria could restrict the granting of US visas The United States government introduced a new guideline that expands the health criteria considered in the evaluation of visas, and now cases of severe obesity may be a reason for refusal, when the visa is for residence in the country. expensive diseases, such as cardiovascular and metabolic diseases, cancer, diabetes, and now obesity considered serious. The new rule adds to other recent changes in the visa process: mandatory in-person interview for all candidates (even children and the elderly), increased application rate and verification of profiles on social networks for student and exchange student visas.
