Introduction to CPF Services

The CPF is an 11-digit number that identifies individual taxpayers in Brazil, managed by the Receita Federal. It is essential for financial, tax, and administrative purposes. Dr. Monique Fernandes, a licensed attorney, offers expert assistance in CPF registration, updates, consultations, and related services.

This guide provides everything you need to know about CPF, including history, structure, purpose, registration process, requirements, updates, consultations, costs, common mistakes, tips, examples, and how professional legal support can streamline the procedure.

History of the CPF

The CPF was created in 1965 by the Receita Federal to identify individual taxpayers. It has evolved to become a key identifier for various services in Brazil.

It is regulated by laws such as Decree 9.723/2019, which made CPF the primary identification number for public services.

Receita Federal CPF History

Purpose and Benefits of the CPF

The CPF is used for tax declaration, banking, employment, voting, accessing public services and most of the things in Brazil. It is mandatory for individuals engaging in financial activities in Brazil or becoming residents.

Benefits include:

  • Unique identification for tax purposes
  • Access to banking and financial services
  • Required for employment, contracts, and needed in general to live in Brazil
  • Facilitates government benefits and programs
  • Enables voting and civic participation
  • Simplifies administrative processes
CPF Overview

How the CPF Works

The CPF is an 11-digit number: XXX.XXX.XXX-XX, where the first 9 digits are sequential, and the last 2 are check digits calculated using a specific algorithm.

Status can be regular, pending regularization, suspended, canceled, or null. It is unique and lifelong for each individual.

Foreigners can obtain CPF for investments or residency.

Requirements for CPF Registration

Brazilian citizens and foreigners residing in Brazil or engaging in activities requiring CPF must register.

Key requirements:

  • Birth certificate or ID for Brazilians
  • Passport (for foreigners)
  • Proof of address
  • Parental consent for minors
  • Power of attorney if applying on behalf
CPF Registration Requirements

CPF for Minors

For minors under 16 years old, registration must be requested by a parent, tutor, or guardian.

Requirements for Minors:

  • Birth certificate or official photo ID of the minor (proving birthplace, parents, and birth date)
  • Official photo ID of the requester (parent, tutor, or guardian)
  • Document proving tutelage or guardianship, if applicable
