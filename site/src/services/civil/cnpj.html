Introduction to CNPJ Services

The CNPJ (Cadastro Nacional da Pessoa Jurídica) is a unique 14-digit identification number assigned to legal entities in Brazil by the Receita Federal. It serves as a national registry for companies, associations, and other entities, essential for tax, business, and legal purposes. Dr. Monique Fernandes, a licensed attorney, offers expert assistance in CNPJ registration, updates, consultations, and related services.

This page provides information about CNPJ, including history, structure, purpose, registration process, requirements, updates, consultations, costs, common mistakes, tips, examples, and how professional legal support can streamline the procedure.

History of the CNPJ

The CNPJ was introduced on July 1, 1998, through Instruction No. SRF 27/1998, evolving from the Cadastro Geral de Contribuintes (CGC) established in 1966 under Laws 4.503/64 and 5.614/70. It is managed by the Receita Federal and regulated by Instruction No. RFB 2119/2022.

From June 2026, new CNPJs will include letters to expand capacity, with letter values based on ASCII minus 48 (e.g., A=17).

Purpose and Benefits of the CNPJ

The CNPJ identifies legal entities for tax and administrative purposes, storing information like opening date, name, economic activities (CNAE), address, and status. It is required for invoices, product packaging, and obligatory business operations.

Benefits include:

  • Unique identification for companies and entities
  • Facilitates tax compliance and administration
  • Enables business transactions and legal operations
  • Supports public data access for transparency
  • Integrates with Redesim for simplified registration
  • Allows enrollment in Simples Nacional for simplified taxation

How the CNPJ Works

The CNPJ is a 14-digit number: XX.XXX.XXX/YYYY-ZZ, where the first 8 digits are the root, YYYY is the branch suffix (0001 for head office), and ZZ is the check digit.

Each establishment shares the root but has a unique suffix. Statuses include Active, Inapt, Suspended, Deactivated, or Null. Validation uses a two-digit check algorithm with specific weights.

From 2026, alphanumeric format will be used for new registrations to expand capacity to over 20 quadrillion possibilities.

Requirements for CNPJ Registration

Entities domiciled in Brazil, including companies, associations, and foreign branches, must register before starting activities. Requirements include approved viability (name, CNAE, address), constitutive act, identification of responsibles, and licenses.

Key requirements:

  • Viability consultation approval
  • Constitutive act (contract, bylaws)
  • Identification of partners/administrators
  • governmental account
  • Operating licenses (sanitary, environmental, fire safety)
  • For MEI: Simplified via Portal do Empreendedor
  • For foreign entities: Additional authorization from DREI
