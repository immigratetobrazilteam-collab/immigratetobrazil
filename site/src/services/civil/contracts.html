Introduction to Contract Services

Contracts are agreements that create, modify, or extinguish legal obligations, as defined in the civil code. Dr. Monique Fernandes, a licensed attorney, provides contract services in compliance with the Brazilian lawyer's statute and the lawyer's code of ethics.

This page explains contract law in Brazil and details the services available, including drafting, reviewing, negotiating, and managing contracts for various clients.

History of Contracts in Brazil

Brazilian contract law is based on the Civil Code, which replaced the 1916 Code and incorporated principles of good faith and social function of contracts. The Código de Defesa do Consumidor (Lei 8.078/1990) added protections for consumer contracts.

Laws like the arbitration and mediation laws have expanded alternative dispute resolution options.

Código Civil Brasileiro

Purpose and Benefits of Contracts

Contracts establish rights and obligations between parties (Código Civil, Article 421). They provide legal certainty and can include mechanisms for dispute resolution.

Benefits include:

  • Enforceability in courts or arbitration
  • Protection of interests through specific clauses
  • Compliance with consumer rights under CDC
  • Data privacy safeguards per LGPD
  • Flexibility for international agreements
Código de Defesa do Consumidor

How Contracts Work in Brazil

Contracts are valid upon consent of capable parties with a lawful object (Código Civil, Article 104). They must adhere to good faith principles.

Enforcement follows the civil litigation code, with options for mediation or arbitration.

Requirements for Valid Contracts

Contracts require capable parties, lawful object, and appropriate form (Código Civil, Article 104).

Additional requirements:

  • Mutual consent
  • Compliance with public policy
  • Data protection
  • Consumer protections
  • Written form for certain agreements

Updating or Canceling Contracts

Amendments require agreement of parties (Código Civil, Article 421).

  1. Assess need for change
  2. Draft amendment
  3. Obtain signatures
  4. Register if required
  5. For cancellation: Follow terms or seek resolution
