What This Service Covers

  • Urgent protection strategy, emergency motions, institutional coordination, and sustained legal follow-up.
  • Bilingual legal guidance (English and Portuguese) with strategic updates.
  • Case monitoring through the agreed administrative or judicial stage.

What Is Not Included

  • Government decisions and processing times outside attorney control.
  • Third-party disbursements and fees unless expressly included.
  • Additional appeals or judicial actions outside contracted scope.

Timeline and Fee Band

Timeline: Immediate action to 24 months

Legal Fee (USD): 1743 to 6780

Legal Fee (BRL): 9000 to 35000

Final fee depends on case complexity and document status. External costs are billed separately.

Documents Often Reviewed

  • Identity records plus any notices, reports, witness statements, or medical documents related to the rights issue.
  • Administrative or court papers already received from authorities, employers, schools, or other institutions.
  • Timeline notes and evidence that help preserve urgency, vulnerability, and requested protective measures.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is it worth booking this consultation?
When you need route selection, document review, filing-risk analysis, or a faster decision on whether this legal path fits your case.

Can timelines or fees change?
Yes. Authority workload, document gaps, foreign-record preparation, and case complexity can change both timeline and final fee.

What should I prepare before the first call?
Bring your passport or ID, key civil records, any authority notices already received, and a short timeline of what has happened so far.
