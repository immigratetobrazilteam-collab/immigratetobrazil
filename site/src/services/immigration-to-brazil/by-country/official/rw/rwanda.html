Umunyamategeko wo muri Berezile ushinzwe abinjira n'abasohoka ku baturage b'u Rwanda

  • Umunyamategeko Monique Fernandes ni umunyamategeko wo muri Berezile wanditswe mu buryo bukurikije amategeko n'Urugaga rw'Abavoka muri Berezile (BAR / OAB).
  • Serivisi zemewe zirimo abinjira, abenegihugu, umuryango, hamwe n’uburenganzira bwa muntu bijyanye na Berezile.
  • Kugisha inama nintambwe yambere mbere yinama zemewe cyangwa guhagararirwa.
  • Inkunga y'Icyongereza n'Igiporutugali irahari kubakiriya muri Berezile ndetse no mumahanga.

Impanuro zemewe n'amategeko Kwimukira muri Berezile kuva mu Rwanda bitangirana no kugisha inama

Mbere yuko Monique Fernandes ashobora gutanga inama mu by'amategeko cyangwa guhagararirwa kwimukira muri Berezile avuye mu Rwanda, birasabwa kugisha inama. Inama yemerera ukuri, igihe, ningamba gusubirwamo neza.

Muganga ntabwo atangira kwivuza mbere yo kubonana. Umunyamategeko ntatangira inama mu by'amategeko mbere yo kugisha inama.

Aho Abanyarwanda Batuye muri Berezile

Nta tegeko na rimwe ryerekana aho abaturage b'u Rwanda batuye muri Berezile. Mubikorwa, ahantu hasanzwe hakurikira inkunga yumuryango, akazi cyangwa amahirwe yo kwiga, ingengo yimari, ikirere gikunda ikirere, nuburyo umukiriya agomba kuba hafi ya konsuline, kwiyandikisha, cyangwa izindi nzego.

Ikiguzi cyo kubaho

Ibiciro byo gutura muri Berezile biratandukanye cyane mumijyi, abaturanyi, uburyo bwo guturamo, guhitamo ishuri, ibikenerwa mu bwikorezi, ndetse no kwimuka bitangira wenyine cyangwa n'umuryango. Kubera iyo mpamvu, kuyobora ibiciro hano biguma muri rusange kandi bifatika aho gutangaza imibare ihamye.

Serivise zemewe n’abaturage b’u Rwanda bimukira muri Berezile

  • Kugereranya inzira ya viza no gutura mbere yo gutanga.
  • Gahunda yo kwimuka ihuza imiryango no guhuza imanza.
  • Kuba umunyagihugu no guteganya imiterere yigihe kirekire nyuma yo gutura muri Berezile.
  • Konseye, guhindura, kwandika, hamwe nuburyo bukurikirana kubibazo byimuka byimuka.
  • Amategeko ajyanye n’imbonezamubano n’umuryango iyo kwimuka biterwa nubukwe, kuvuka, cyangwa izindi nyandiko zambuka imipaka.

Yego. Isubiramo ryambere ryamategeko rifasha gusobanura inzira nyayo, ibibazo byingenzi byigihe, nintambwe ikomeye ikomeye ikurikira mbere yuko wiyemeza gukora ingendo cyangwa gutanga.

Abakiriya benshi basaba kugisha inama mbere yo gutanga kugirango inzira yahisemo ihuye nintego nyayo, ibintu birimo, nigihe ntarengwa.

Yego. Ubuyobozi bwambere, gusubiramo inzira, no gutegura ingamba birashobora gutangira kure mbere yo kwimukira muri Berezile.

Zana intego yawe nyamukuru, aho uri, urugendo cyangwa gutanga igihe ntarengwa, hamwe nincamake yerekana uko umuryango wawe umeze n'inzira urimo utekereza.
