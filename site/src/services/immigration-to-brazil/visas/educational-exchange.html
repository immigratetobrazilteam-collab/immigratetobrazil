Introduction to Brazil Educational Exchange Visa

The Educational Exchange Visa to visit Brazil, also known as VITEM IV, is a temporary visa category that permits foreign students and participants in exchange programs to engage for long time in educational activities in Brazil and also allows the student to work. This visa is ruled by the Brazilian migratory legislation, allowing participation in events such as classes, research, and academic exchanges.

This is general information based on current legislation and is not legal advice. Laws may change; consult a qualified professional for specific cases.

Who Qualifies for Educational Exchange Visa

  • Foreign people that wish to study in Brazil.
  • Students can enrol in a variety of courses, like medical observation course as part of their post graduation abroad; internship; research internship; school program; university courses; language.
  • Eligibility depends on compliance with migratory norms and the activity's contribution to educational exchange or public benefit.

Criteria are subject to official evaluation; this is not exhaustive.

Benefits and Opportunities

  • Visa holders may participate in educational classes, research exchange, and collaborative projects in Brazil.
  • Access to opportunities such as the PEC-G program, academic fairs, networking events, workshops, and partnerships with Brazilian institutions while also being alowed to work.
  • Brazil offers a dynamic educational environment with events and institutions that support professional development.

Any benefits are governed by applicable laws and individual circumstances.
