Introduction to Skilled Worker Residency

Brazil provides residency pathways for skilled foreign workers with job offers from Brazilian companies or in high-demand sectors.

This is general information based on current legislation and is not legal advice. Laws may change; consult a qualified professional for specific cases.

  • Access to social security and labor rights.
  • Path to permanent residency.
