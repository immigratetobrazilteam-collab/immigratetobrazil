Legal Foundations for Expulsion Defense in Brazil

Brazilian Legislation

Expulsion is regulated by migration law and is applied mainly in cases of final criminal convictions for serious offences, while guaranteeing the right to defence, appeals, and humanitarian protections for those with strong family or personal ties to Brazil..

Official sources: Law 13.445/2017; Decree 9.199/2017; Ministry of Justice.

Key Aspects of Expulsion Defense in Brazil

Defense Overview

Legal opposition to expulsion decrees, leveraging due process, humanitarian grounds, and procedural defenses to avoid removal.

Client Rights

  • Notification and defense opportunity.
  • Administrative and judicial appeals.
  • Humanitarian non-expulsion in certain cases.
  • Access to legal aid.

Overview of Expulsion Defense Services

Administrative Defense

Submitting defenses to the Ministry of Justice against expulsion decrees.

Judicial Defense

Filing habeas corpus or other actions in federal courts to block expulsion.

Why Professional Defense is Crucial

Complex Procedures

Involves multiple governmental levels and strict deadlines.

High Stakes

Expulsion leads to long-term re-entry bans.
