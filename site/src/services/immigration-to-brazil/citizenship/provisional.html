Provisional Naturalization FAQ

Provisional naturalization is a form of Brazilian citizenship granted to minors who come to live in Brazil and become legal residents before turning 10 years old.

Minors born abroad and legally residing in Brazil before turning 10 years old are eligible for provisional naturalization.

Provisional naturalization is processed by the Ministry of Justice.

Provisional naturalization can be applied for minors after they get their resident status.

Provisional naturalization provides Brazilian citizenship to children, granting rights like passport and many other rights.

Register as a legal resident in Brazil, submit documents proving residency, and await provisional citizenship grant, with obligation to confirm upon majority.

Resident registration is required for provisional naturalization, granting citizenship with confirmation obligation at age 18.

For minors, criminal background checks are generally not required.

Even if all legal requirements are met, the authorities have discretion to assess factors such as conduct or legal compliance. Denials must be justified, and applicants have the right to appeal.
