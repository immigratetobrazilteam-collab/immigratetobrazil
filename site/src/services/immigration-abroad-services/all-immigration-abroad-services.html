FAQ

Common questions before starting an international move

Can attorney Monique Fernandes compare more than one destination country?

Yes. The service can begin with a comparison of the countries being considered, the objective behind the move, and the type of immigration pathway that may be relevant in each jurisdiction.

What should be shared when comparing countries?

It helps to mention the countries under consideration, the reason for relocating, and the available records so the consultation can focus on the right legal variables.

Why does attorney Monique Fernandes ask about family, work, or study plans?

Those facts usually define the immigration route being evaluated, the documents that may be needed, and which destination service should be reviewed first.
