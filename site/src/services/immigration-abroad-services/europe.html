Rights and Obligations

Rights of Residents

  • Legal Protection: Access to legal systems and protection under European and national laws
  • Residential Rights: Freedom of movement within Schengen Area and access to housing markets
  • Employment Rights: Authorization to work and engage in professional activities across member states
  • Family Rights: Ability to bring family members under applicable regulations and directives
  • Educational Access: Rights to education and training opportunities across European institutions
  • Healthcare Access: Access to medical services and health systems in member countries

Obligations of Residents

  • Legal Compliance: Adherence to all national laws and European Union regulations
  • Documentation: Maintenance of valid immigration documents and residence permits
  • Financial Responsibility: Meeting financial obligations and self-sufficiency requirements
  • Social Integration: Respect for local customs and community standards
  • Timely Reporting: Compliance with reporting and renewal requirements
  • Tax Obligations: Fulfillment of applicable tax and fiscal responsibilities

Rights and obligations vary by country and visa type. As your immigration attorney, I ensure you understand and fulfill all legal requirements while maximizing your rights.
