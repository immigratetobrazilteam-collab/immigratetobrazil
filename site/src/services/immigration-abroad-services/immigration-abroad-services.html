Immigration Abroad Services

  • Africa Immigration

    Dr. Monique Fernandes offers expert guidance on visa applications, residency permits, and citizenship processes for various African countries, ensuring compliance with local immigration laws and providing support for work, study, or family reunification visas.

  • Asia Immigration

    Dr. Monique Fernandes provides comprehensive assistance with Asian immigration, including skilled worker visas, student visas, and permanent residency applications, tailored to countries like Japan, Singapore, and China, with focus on documentation and appeals.

  • Europe Immigration

    Dr. Monique Fernandes specializes in European immigration services, offering support for Schengen visas, EU Blue Card applications, and citizenship by investment programs, with expertise in countries like Portugal, Spain, and Germany.

  • MERCOSUL Immigration

    Dr. Monique Fernandes assists with MERCOSUL immigration, including temporary and permanent residencies for member countries like Argentina, Paraguay, and Uruguay, facilitating work permits and family reunifications under regional agreements.

  • UK Immigration

    Dr. Monique Fernandes offers UK immigration services, including skilled worker visas, student visas, and family sponsorships, with guidance on post-Brexit requirements and settlement applications.

  • USA Immigration

    Dr. Monique Fernandes provides USA immigration support, including EB visas, family-based petitions, and naturalization processes, with expertise in USCIS applications and appeals.
