Overview

When a Brazilian resides in another country and works there, without earnings coming from Brazil, they may have to declare and be taxed in both parents, or just in the country where they are. Brazilians can be considered tax residents in both countries or just in the country where they are living, if they declare their departure from Brazil. Brazil has double taxation agreements with several countries, for example with Portugal. As well, it also has reciprocity for tax compensation with some countries such as the USA. According to the tax agreement between Brazil and Portugal, if a Brazilian resident in Portugal is subject to taxation on his income in Portugal, at the time of declaring Brazilian income tax, he may be exempt from paying taxes on the same income in Brazil, as long as he meets the conditions established in the agreement.
