Overview

What is a dating contract? If the couple considers themselves as a family, acts as if they were married, has a public, long-lasting and continuous relationship, then this relationship can be considered a Stable Union. With the Stable Union taking place without defining the property regime, all assets acquired during the relationship will belong to both parties, regardless of who made the payment or if the asset was registered in just one name. This way, in the event of an end to the relationship, they will have to divide the assets. If lovers want to avoid a Stable Union, a Dating Contract can be drawn up.
