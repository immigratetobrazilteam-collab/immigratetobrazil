Overview

If you are an investor looking to reside in Brazil with foreign resources, purchasing property in Brazil can indeed lead to the possibility to apply for an investor visa. It mainly requires a minimum investment of R$ 1,000,000 in property as part of the criteria. There is also an opportunity for a discount of up to 30% on the minimum investment for properties located in the Northern and Northeastern regions of Brazil. This visa allows for a residence period of up to 4 years, which can later be renewed or converted into permanent residency. However, it’s important to note that this category is specifically for real estate investment and does not apply to other forms of investment, such as business or technology ventures. Legal guidance can help clarify the applicable rules, the risks involved, and the next step that best fits your situation.
