Overview

During the naturalization process, the applicant will be called for a personal interview, which takes place at the Federal Police unit in the region where he or she lives. This interview aims to verify data, aptitude in the Portuguese language, integration into Brazilian society and the absence of criminal records that could disqualify the granting of the request. Attendance at this stage is mandatory and is part of due legal process. However, there are reports of interviews carried out outside the official premises of the Federal Police, including at the applicant's home, by agents who present themselves without a judicial warrant. It should be clarified that the applicant is not legally obliged to allow state agents to enter his residence without a specific court order, as guaranteed by article 5, item XI, of the Federal Constitution. Possible entry permission, even under the pretext of mere administrative diligence, may expose the individual to undue inspections and the collection of elements unrelated to the objective of the interview. It should be noted that the legislation does not provide for a home interview to be carried out during the naturalization process, and such an administrative act must take place in a location that ensures the privacy, dignity and confidentiality of the applicant.
