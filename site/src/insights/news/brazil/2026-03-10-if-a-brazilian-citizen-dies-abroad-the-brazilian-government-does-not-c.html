Overview

If a Brazilian citizen dies abroad, the Brazilian government does not cover funeral expenses or transportation of the body to Brazil. However, the Brazilian consulate in the country of death can advise on the necessary procedures, such as issuing the death certificate and releasing the body. The consular death certificate must be registered at a registry office in Brazil to be legally valid. If the death has not been registered at the consulate, the foreign certificate must be apostilled or legalized, translated by a sworn translator and registered in Brazil. Missing information, such as cause of death, can be added later without the need for a court decision.
