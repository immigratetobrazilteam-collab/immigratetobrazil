Overview

The company hired me as MEI. Do I have labor rights? 1. Pejotization occurs when the worker is forced to form a PJ to be hired, in order to disguise the employment relationship. There is the false promise that by receiving a higher remuneration, the costs and taxes imposed by law would be lower. 2. Thus, the company stops making some mandatory payments and the worker loses the protection and benefits of labor law and social security. You can only be hired as a PJ if you are going to provide services independently. 3. If you have fixed remuneration and hours, continuous services, achievement of goals and subordination to the employer, then it may be a labor fraud and the right to have the employment relationship recognized in court arises. If you are being coerced or forced to create a PJ to be hired, report it to the Public Ministry of Labor.
