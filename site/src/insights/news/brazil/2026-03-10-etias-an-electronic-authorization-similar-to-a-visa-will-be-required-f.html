Overview

ETIAS, an electronic authorization similar to a visa, will be required for tourists visiting Europe from May 2025, as announced by the European Union. Travelers from visa-exempt countries, such as Brazil, will need to pay a fee of 7 euros to obtain the authorization, which will be valid for three years or until the passport expires. Minors and those over 70 years of age will be exempt from paying the fee. In addition to document and database verification, there will be fingerprint scanning and facial recognition during visa processing. This new electronic registration system will replace the old stamps in the passport. Visa checks will take place not only at airports, but also at ports and train stations in Europe. Requests must be processed within 96 hours.
