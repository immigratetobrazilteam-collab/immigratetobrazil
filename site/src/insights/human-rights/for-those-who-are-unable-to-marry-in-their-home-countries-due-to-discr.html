Overview

For those who are unable to marry in their home countries due to discrimination, Brazil offers a beacon of hope. LGBTQIA+ couples can come to Brazil to celebrate their love or even get married through a proxy marriage if traveling is currently not possible. This means you can get married in Brazil without ever coming to Brazil. Since May 2013, Brazil's Federal Supreme Court has legally allowed and recognized same-gender marriages. This groundbreaking decision has brought immense joy and relief to countless LGBTQIA+ couples seeking the rights and recognition they deserve. For LGBTQIA+ individuals facing discrimination or legal obstacles preventing them from marrying in their home countries, Brazil provides a promising solution. By traveling to Brazil, couples can celebrate their love in a country where their union will be respected and legally acknowledged, regardless of whether they or their partners are Brazilian or nationals of another country. Recognizing the challenges faced by individuals who cannot travel to Brazil, the country's legal system allows Marriage by Proxy. This provision enables one or both partners, with the assistance of an attorney, to represent themselves during the marriage application process and ceremony. By appointing an attorney in Brazil, couples can fulfill their dreams of a legally binding marriage, even if they are physically unable to be present. If you would like to get married, or know someone who needs to get married in Brazil, contact a lawyer today. An immigration lawyer understands the importance of complying with immigration regulations and can help you navigate the process of obtaining the necessary documentation for your immigration journey. Legal guidance can help clarify the applicable rules, the risks involved, and the next step that best fits your situation.
