Overview

A foreigner person residing in Brazil may have the obligation to declare taxes both in Brazil and in their home country, depending on the tax laws of each country and the type of income they receive. In Brazil, residents are generally taxed based on their global income, meaning they must declare both income obtained in Brazil and abroad. However, there are bilateral agreements between Brazil and several countries that can help avoid double taxation on the same income. When there is no bilateral agreement with the country, Brazil analyzes reciprocity and may offset when the Brazilian income declaration is made. On the other hand, in their home country, the foreigner may have tax obligations with different rules depending on the local tax laws. Immigrating to a new country is a life-altering decision, and seeking professional guidance can significantly impact the success of your journey.
