Why consultation is required

Legal advice and representation start with consultation

Before an attorney can provide legal advice or represent a client, a consultation is necessary. The consultation allows Monique Fernandes to understand the facts, review the legal issue carefully, and define the best legal strategy for the situation presented.

A helpful comparison is medical care. A doctor does not begin treatment before an appointment. A lawyer does not begin legal advice before consultation. Each legal matter has its own facts, timing, risks, and strategic implications.

Understand the objective

The consultation clarifies what you are trying to achieve and what legal route may fit best.

Review the legal issues

Similar situations can still require different strategies once the facts, timing, and risks are reviewed carefully.

Protect the professional process

The consultation helps confirm there is no conflict of interest and that the matter can be handled responsibly.

Define the next step

The goal is not only to answer questions, but to identify the strongest next legal move for your specific situation.
