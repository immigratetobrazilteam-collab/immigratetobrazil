Latest Updates in This Hub

  • When a person from abroad arrives in Brazil, this person has the right to drive in Brazil for the first 180 days if there is an international legal agreement or reciprocity between the country of origin and Brazil. (2026-03-09)
  • When a Brazilian resides in another country and works there, without earnings coming from Brazil, they may have to declare and be taxed in both parents, or just in the country where they are. (2026-03-09)
  • The revalidation of foreign diplomas is a mandatory process for professionals who have graduated abroad to validate their academic qualifications and practise their professions in Brazil. (2026-03-09)
  • The revalidation of a diploma obtained abroad is a mandatory process so that professionals trained outside Brazil can validate their academic qualifications and practice their professions in the country. (2026-03-09)
  • Registration for the first 2024 edition of the exam to obtain the Certificate of Proficiency in Portuguese Language for Foreigners (Celpe-Bras) is open. (2026-03-09)
  • It is important to organize your tourism/business trip, be prepared and have documents on hand. (2026-03-09)
