Overview

Home page introducing immigration pathways, advisory boundaries, consultation flow, and cross-linked route families.

The site is structured to give readers clear guidance, grounded in official sources and neutral language, before any consultation is requested.

People exploring immigration to Brazil often need a single page that brings together concept, procedure, documentation logic, and authority context. Throughout the site, the language stays informational, avoids guarantees, and points readers back to official sources whenever a formal rule or authority decision controls the next step.

Consultation flow

  1. Submit the consultation request with your current situation, goals, and any available documents.
  2. Pay the consultation fee using PayPal, Wise, PIX, or Payoneer to immigratetobrazilteam@gmail.com.
  3. Send proof of payment by email or WhatsApp so the team can review the request manually.
  4. Wait for confirmation. Appointments are only scheduled at least 36 hours after payment confirmation.

Official resources

Primary government or institutional sources referenced when framing this page.

Primary statutory framework for migration, admission, residence, rights, duties, and administrative measures in Brazil.

Migration Regulation Decree (Decree No. 9.199/2017)

Regulatory decree used to interpret procedures, authorities, documentation logic, and migration administration.

Policia Federal immigration services

Federal Police portal for registration, migration documentation, and post-arrival obligations.

Institutional source for professional regulation, ethics, and legal profession standards in Brazil.
