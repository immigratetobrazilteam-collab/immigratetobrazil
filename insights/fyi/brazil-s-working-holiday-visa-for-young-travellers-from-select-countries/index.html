Overview

Brazil's Working Holiday Visa For young travellers from select countries, Brazil's Temporary Working Holiday Visa offers a unique opportunity to combine leisure exploration with temporary employment, fostering cultural exchange and personal growth. Granted on the basis of reciprocity through bilateral agreements, this visa is currently available to citizens of New Zealand and France, allowing stays of up to one year for individuals typically aged 18 to 30, to work and fund their adventures while immersing themselves in Brazilian life. Eligibility requires meeting specific criteria, such as sufficient funds, health insurance, and no dependent children accompanying the applicant. It is advisable to consult an immigration lawyer to review your circumstances and guide you through the application process in accordance with prevailing regulations. -
