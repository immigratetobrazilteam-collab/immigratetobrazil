LGPD compliance statement

LGPD compliance statement

Immigrate to Brazil approaches personal-data treatment on the basis that it must be lawful, purpose-driven, proportionate, and transparent. This page supplements our Privacy Policy by expressing that framework in Brazil-first compliance language.

Privacy compliance in this context is not only a question of disclosure. It is a question of how intake, communication, document handling, and service records are governed day to day.

Section 02

Identification of the controlador

For the purposes of this notice, Immigrate to Brazil acts as the controlador of the personal-data processing described on this page in connection with the platform, forms, communications, consultation handling, payment verification, and internal operational recordkeeping.

LGPD-related contact should be directed to immigratetobrazilteam@gmail.com.

Section 03

Data processing purpose

Personal data may be processed for route assessment, chronology review, communication, document planning, service delivery, compliance controls, fraud prevention, payment matching, and protection of rights in operational, administrative, or legal contexts.

The principle is purpose limitation. Data is processed because the service model requires it for a legitimate function.

Section 04

Legal bases under Brazilian law

Depending on the situation, legal bases may include consent where appropriate, legal or regulatory obligation, procedures preliminary to contract formation, contract execution, regular exercise of rights, legitimate interests where lawful and proportionate, and protection of life or physical safety where relevant.

The correct legal basis depends on what is actually being done with the information, not on which phrase appears most convenient.

Section 05

Brazilian user rights

Under the LGPD, data subjects may have rights including confirmation of processing, access to data, correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, anonymization, blocking or deletion in cases recognized by law, portability where applicable, information about sharing, information about the consequences of denying consent, and revocation of consent where consent is the legal basis.

Requests should be specific enough to support a controlled review and, where appropriate, identity verification.

Section 06

Data retention and sharing

Data is retained only as long as necessary for legal, operational, contractual, evidentiary, or compliance purposes. Information may be shared with service providers, collaborators, legal professionals, translation providers, or authorities where necessary for lawful operations or service execution.

Sharing remains tied to purpose and necessity, not casual convenience.

Section 07

ANPD reference

If a person believes LGPD rights have not been respected, that person may review ANPD guidance and the ANPD petition route in addition to contacting us directly. The ANPD remains the principal public authority reference within the Brazilian data-protection framework.

Section 08

Contact for requests

LGPD-related requests should be sent to immigratetobrazilteam@gmail.com.
