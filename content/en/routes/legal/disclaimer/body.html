Information is general in nature

Information provided by Immigrate to Brazil is general in nature and is intended to support orientation, understanding, and structured decision-making.

Content may explain:

  • immigration pathways and route logic
  • documentation expectations
  • procedural structure and sequencing
  • common risk factors and practical considerations

However, such information does not constitute a complete or definitive answer for any specific case.

Immigration matters are inherently fact-sensitive. The same legal framework may produce different outcomes depending on:

  • personal chronology
  • nationality
  • prior filings or refusals
  • family or relationship structure
  • documentation quality
  • authority-specific procedures

For this reason, general information should not be relied upon as a substitute for case-specific analysis.

Section 02

Not a substitute for individualized legal advice

Nothing provided through Immigrate to Brazil should be interpreted as individualized legal advice unless it has been delivered within a properly defined professional context.

Individualized advice requires:

  • controlled review of relevant facts
  • analysis of supporting records
  • clearly defined service scope
  • appropriate professional engagement

Public content, messages, or preliminary exchanges may clarify general concepts but do not resolve a legal matter.

Users should not rely on general information alone when making decisions that carry legal or procedural consequences.

Section 03

No guarantee of approval or outcome

Immigrate to Brazil does not guarantee:

  • visa approval
  • residency grant or renewal
  • naturalization or nationality recognition
  • registration or regularization outcomes
  • acceptance of documentation
  • favorable administrative or judicial decisions

All such outcomes are determined exclusively by government authorities or competent bodies.

Any suggestion that a private service can guarantee an immigration outcome would misrepresent the role of authority discretion.

Fees are charged for professional services, not for the outcome of a process.

Section 04

Government authorities control outcomes

Government authorities, registries, consulates, and courts retain full control over:

  • interpretation of legal requirements
  • assessment of documentation
  • procedural acceptance or rejection
  • issuance, registration, or recognition decisions
  • processing timelines

Immigrate to Brazil may assist in structuring, organizing, and supporting a process, but does not replace or override public authority decision-making.

This distinction is central to responsible immigration practice.

Section 05

Timelines are variable and external

Any reference to timelines, durations, or expected processing periods should be understood as indicative guidance only.

Actual timelines depend on factors outside the control of Immigrate to Brazil, including:

  • authority workload and internal processes
  • document issuance timelines from third parties
  • translation and certification requirements
  • filing sequence and procedural order
  • regulatory or policy changes
  • technical or administrative delays

No guarantee is made regarding processing speed or completion time.

Planning should account for variability.

Section 06

Representation boundary

Use of the Immigrate to Brazil website or services does not, by itself, create a formal professional relationship.

This includes:

  • visiting the site
  • submitting a form
  • sending a message
  • participating in an initial consultation
  • making payment for an early-stage service

A professional engagement begins only when:

  • the scope of work is clearly defined
  • the engagement is accepted
  • where applicable, a written agreement is executed

This boundary exists to prevent incorrect assumptions regarding responsibility, scope, and representation.

Section 07

Relationship with legal representation and governing contracts

Where legal representation is required or provided, it is carried out by a qualified lawyer under a separate and specific agreement.

That agreement governs:

  • the scope of legal services
  • professional obligations
  • fees and payment terms
  • liability and responsibility

In the event of any inconsistency between this Disclaimer and a lawyer-client agreement, the lawyer-client agreement prevails with respect to legal services.

Immigrate to Brazil does not replace, override, or modify the professional responsibilities of licensed legal practitioners.

Section 08

Third-party information and external dependencies

Immigration processes often involve third parties, including:

  • government authorities
  • registries and public offices
  • translation and certification providers
  • external service systems

Information relating to such third parties may be provided for orientation purposes. However:

  • third-party processes are not controlled by Immigrate to Brazil
  • external requirements may change without notice
  • third-party timelines and standards may vary

Immigrate to Brazil is not responsible for the actions, delays, or decisions of third parties.

Section 09

Limitation of reliance

Users are responsible for evaluating how they use the information provided.

Decisions made solely on the basis of general content, without proper case-specific review, are made at the user's own risk.

Immigrate to Brazil does not accept responsibility for outcomes resulting from:

  • reliance on general information without consultation
  • incomplete or inaccurate user assumptions
  • actions taken outside a defined service scope

This limitation exists to reflect the complexity and variability of immigration matters.

Section 10

Updates and accuracy of information

Immigration laws, policies, and procedures may change over time.

While reasonable efforts are made to maintain accurate and up-to-date information, Immigrate to Brazil does not guarantee that all content reflects the most current legal or administrative position at all times.

Users should seek updated, case-specific guidance where accuracy is critical.
