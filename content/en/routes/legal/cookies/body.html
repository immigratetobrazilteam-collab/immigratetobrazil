Purpose of cookies in platform operation

Purpose of cookies in platform operation

Cookies and similar technologies are used as part of the technical and operational framework of the platform. Their role is not uniform and should not be treated as such.

Certain cookies are strictly necessary to enable the platform to function in a stable, secure, and accessible manner. These may support session continuity, navigation behavior, consent recording, accessibility configurations, and core technical processes explicitly requested by the user.

Other technologies may be used to observe how the platform performs in practice. This includes identifying structural friction points, navigation inefficiencies, and areas where usability or clarity can be improved.

Where additional technologies are used for optional features or integrations, their function must be clearly separated from essential operations. Each category of cookies serves a distinct purpose and is governed accordingly.

The platform does not treat all cookies as interchangeable. Their use is structured, purpose-bound, and proportionate to the function they support.

Section 02

Categories of cookies used

Cookies and similar technologies are organized into functional categories to ensure clarity, control, and appropriate governance.

The platform distinguishes between:

  • essential cookies
  • analytical cookies
  • optional preference or marketing cookies
  • third-party integration cookies

This categorization is not merely descriptive. It reflects differences in necessity, legal basis, and consent requirements.

Essential cookies are tied to core platform functionality and are treated differently from optional technologies. Analytical and optional cookies are subject to stricter control, particularly where user consent is required under applicable data protection frameworks.

Section 03

Essential cookies

Essential cookies are required for the proper operation of the platform and are directly linked to services or functionalities explicitly requested by the user.

These may include:

  • session management and navigation continuity
  • security-related controls and abuse prevention
  • consent recording and preference storage
  • accessibility-related configurations
  • technical infrastructure required for page delivery

These cookies do not operate for profiling or marketing purposes. Their function is limited to ensuring that the platform behaves as expected in a secure and consistent manner.

Because of their operational necessity, essential cookies may be deployed without prior consent where permitted by applicable law. However, their use remains limited to what is strictly required for functionality.

Disabling essential cookies at the browser level may result in degraded performance, restricted access to certain features, or complete inability to use parts of the platform.

Section 04

Analytical cookies

Analytical cookies are used to understand how the platform performs in real-world conditions and how users interact with its structure.

This may include:

  • page interaction patterns
  • navigation flow between sections
  • time spent on pages
  • identification of technical issues or performance bottlenecks

The purpose of analytical processing is limited to improving usability, clarity, and system performance. It is not intended to create individual user profiles or to support behavioral targeting.

Where possible, analytical data is aggregated, anonymized, or otherwise minimized to reduce the impact on individual privacy.

The use of analytical cookies is subject to applicable legal requirements. Where consent is required, these cookies will only be activated after such consent has been obtained.

Section 05

Optional cookies and tracking systems

Optional cookies include technologies that are not required for the operation of the platform but may enhance functionality, personalization, or communication.

These may include:

  • preference-based features
  • marketing or remarketing technologies
  • extended tracking tools beyond basic analytics

Optional means optional. The refusal of these technologies must not prevent access to core legal information, consultation pathways, or essential service functionality.

Such cookies will only be activated where a valid consent mechanism has been provided and accepted by the user.

The platform does not deploy optional tracking technologies by default. Activation occurs only after an affirmative user action where required by law.

Section 06

Third-party integrations

The platform may incorporate third-party tools or services that rely on their own cookies or similar technologies.

These may include:

  • analytics providers
  • embedded communication tools
  • scheduling systems
  • external content integrations

Where such tools are used, their function is evaluated in relation to the overall platform structure. Their presence does not remove the obligation to ensure transparency, proportionality, and proper consent handling.

Third-party providers may process data according to their own policies. However, their integration into the platform is subject to internal review and alignment with applicable data protection principles.

Users should be aware that interacting with third-party features may result in data being processed outside the direct control of the platform.

Section 07

User control mechanisms

Users retain control over the use of cookies through multiple mechanisms.

These include:

  • browser-level settings allowing deletion, restriction, or blocking of cookies
  • device-level privacy configurations
  • any consent or preference management interface provided on the platform

Browser-level controls operate independently of the platform. If cookies are restricted or blocked at this level, certain functionalities may be affected.

This may include:

  • loss of saved preferences
  • reduced accessibility behavior
  • interruptions in session continuity
  • limited functionality in certain areas

Such outcomes should be understood as a direct result of user-configured restrictions rather than an error or malfunction of the platform.

Where consent mechanisms are implemented, users may update or withdraw their preferences at any time, subject to technical limitations.

Section 08

Relationship with the Privacy Policy

This Cookies Policy operates as a specific extension of the broader data governance framework described in the Privacy Policy.

The Privacy Policy addresses:

  • categories of personal data processed
  • legal bases for processing
  • data sharing and international transfers
  • user rights under applicable law

This page focuses exclusively on the role of cookies and similar technologies within that framework.

Both documents should be read together to obtain a complete understanding of how data is handled across the platform.

Section 09

Updates and version control

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect:

  • changes in technology
  • updates in legal or regulatory requirements
  • modifications to platform structure or services

The most current version will always be available on this page. Continued use of the platform after updates may indicate acceptance of the revised terms, where applicable.
