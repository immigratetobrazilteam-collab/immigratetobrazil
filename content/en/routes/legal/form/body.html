Overview

A detailed intake form for sending the first summary of your matter.

The form is designed to collect the facts that usually change route analysis, timing, and document planning. That makes the first review more efficient and reduces avoidable follow-up questions.

Submission on its own does not create representation. What it does create is a cleaner starting record, which helps the team identify the likely route, the missing information, and the right next operational step.

A typical work-related file starts with the real activity in Brazil, the sponsoring company or business structure, and the category that best matches that activity before any supporting record is finalized.

Typical records include passports, corporate records, contracts or assignment material, proof of the proposed activity in Brazil, and any documents the sponsor must issue. Many avoidable problems begin when the visa label is chosen first and the factual structure is checked later.
