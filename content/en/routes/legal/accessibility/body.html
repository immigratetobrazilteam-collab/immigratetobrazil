Commitment to accessibility standards

Monique Fernandes wants legal information and service pathways to be reasonably usable.

That includes page reading, navigation, forms, and contact routes used by people seeking immigration help for Brazil.

Section 02

Accessibility standards and design approach

Accessibility should be considered as part of site design, not as an afterthought.

The site may use recognized accessibility principles and practical adjustments intended to improve readability, structure, navigation, and interaction.

Section 03

Ongoing improvements

Accessibility work is ongoing.

The site may be updated over time to improve usability, remove barriers, and respond to issues reported by visitors.

Section 04

Compatibility with assistive technologies

Compatibility can depend on the device, browser, and technology being used.

Monique aims for practical compatibility, but different assistive tools and environments may produce different results, especially where third-party components are involved.

Section 05

Third-party content and limitations

Not every accessibility issue is controlled entirely by the practice.

Some barriers may involve embedded tools, browser behavior, or third-party systems. Where that happens, Monique can still review the issue and try to help users find a workable alternative.

Section 06

User responsibilities and environment considerations

The user's own environment can affect how the site behaves.

Device settings, browser choice, zoom level, assistive tools, and connectivity can all affect the experience. Those factors do not remove the practice's responsibilities, but they do matter in troubleshooting.

Section 07

Reporting accessibility issues

A clear report helps Monique understand the barrier faster.

Users should explain the page involved, the problem encountered, the device or browser used if known, and the task they were trying to complete.

Section 08

Alternative access and support

If a barrier blocks progress, Monique should try to offer a practical alternative where possible.

That may include providing information through another channel, clarifying the next step directly, or helping the person complete a blocked action in another way.

Section 09

Updates to this statement

This statement may change as the site and the accessibility approach evolve.

The published version is the working reference for the site's accessibility statement at that time.
