Commitment to accessibility standards

Commitment to accessibility standards

Immigrate to Brazil is committed to making its website and related digital services usable by as many people as possible, including individuals who rely on assistive technologies or alternative interaction methods.

This includes users who access content through:

  • screen readers
  • keyboard-only navigation
  • zoom and text-resizing tools
  • high-contrast or custom display settings
  • reduced-motion preferences
  • voice control or other assistive input systems

Accessibility is treated as an operational requirement, not a decorative feature. It directly affects whether a user can:

  • understand the structure and content of the platform
  • navigate between sections without confusion
  • complete forms and submit information
  • access consultation pathways and support

The objective is to reduce unnecessary barriers and ensure that access to immigration-related information and services is not limited by avoidable technical or structural obstacles.

Section 02

Accessibility standards and design approach

Immigrate to Brazil uses recognized accessibility principles as a reference for design, development, and ongoing improvement.

We aim to align, where practical and proportionate, with:

  • Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA

This includes attention to:

  • semantic HTML structure and heading hierarchy
  • readable typography and spacing
  • sufficient color contrast
  • predictable navigation patterns
  • clear labeling of forms and inputs
  • logical tab order for keyboard navigation
  • avoidance of unnecessary motion or visual disruption

Accessibility decisions are made with a focus on clarity, consistency, and usability, rather than superficial compliance.

Section 03

Ongoing improvements

Accessibility is not treated as a fixed or completed state.

We periodically review and refine:

  • navigation logic and menu structure
  • heading hierarchy and page organization
  • form usability and error handling
  • contrast behavior and visual clarity
  • keyboard accessibility
  • content readability and structure

The objective is to identify and reduce friction over time.

Immigrate to Brazil does not represent the platform as fully optimized in all circumstances. Instead, it adopts a model of continuous responsibility and incremental improvement.

Section 04

Compatibility with assistive technologies

The platform is designed to function across commonly used environments, including:

  • modern web browsers (such as Chrome, Safari, Edge, and Firefox)
  • standard desktop and mobile operating systems
  • widely used assistive technologies (such as screen readers and keyboard navigation tools)

Compatibility is influenced by factors outside direct control, including:

  • browser version and configuration
  • device type and performance
  • assistive technology setup
  • third-party integrations embedded within the site

While reasonable efforts are made to ensure compatibility, variation in user experience may occur due to these external factors.

Section 05

Third-party content and limitations

Certain features or services integrated into the website may rely on third-party providers, including:

  • scheduling tools
  • communication interfaces
  • embedded external content

These components may not always fully align with the same accessibility standards applied to the core site.

Where such limitations exist:

  • they are not intentionally introduced
  • they are reviewed where possible
  • alternatives may be offered when a barrier is identified

Third-party involvement does not remove the commitment to accessibility but may affect the degree of control over specific elements.

Section 06

User responsibilities and environment considerations

Accessibility is a shared interaction between platform design and user environment.

Users may improve accessibility by:

  • enabling accessibility features on their device or browser
  • using updated software and assistive tools
  • adjusting display settings (zoom, contrast, text size)

Some accessibility outcomes depend on user configuration rather than platform limitation.

Section 07

Reporting accessibility issues

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, we encourage you to report it.

Please include:

  • the page or section where the issue occurred
  • a description of the issue
  • the task you were attempting to complete
  • any assistive technology or device context, if relevant

Email: immigratetobrazilteam@gmail.com

Reports are reviewed as part of our ongoing accessibility process.

Section 08

Alternative access and support

If an accessibility issue prevents you from completing a specific action, such as submitting a form, booking a consultation, or accessing information, we will, where reasonably possible:

  • provide an alternative method of access
  • assist with completing the requested step manually
  • ensure continuity of communication

The objective is to prevent accessibility barriers from blocking access to essential services.

Section 09

Updates to this statement

This Accessibility Statement may be updated periodically to reflect:

  • improvements to the platform
  • changes in accessibility standards
  • updates to integrated tools or services

The current version published on the website represents the applicable statement.
