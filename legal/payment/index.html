Overview

Consultation payments should be clear, traceable, and easy to confirm.

Consultations and agreed services may be paid through PIX, PayPal, Payoneer, Wise, direct bank transfer, Bitcoin, or USDT. The method used should match the written payment instructions shared for your booking or enquiry.

After payment, send proof by email or WhatsApp so the transfer can be matched to your enquiry. Payment is not treated as a confirmed appointment until it has been verified and confirmed manually.

Operational payment guidance 01

Consultation payment explanation

How payment works for consultations and agreed service stages.

Payment begins the confirmation process for the consultation or agreed service stage, but it does not by itself reserve a time slot.

A booking is only treated as confirmed once the transfer has been verified, matched to the enquiry, and confirmed manually in writing.

Why it matters Payment and booking are separate steps.
Best next step Pay using the correct method, then send proof immediately.

Operational payment guidance 02

Payment methods

Choose the method that is safest and easiest to verify.

Use the payment method that is most practical for your location, currency, and transaction type. If a method requires prior written details, wait for those details before sending funds.

If you are unsure which route is appropriate, ask before paying. It is better to clarify first than to correct a payment issue later.

Why it matters A clear payment trail avoids delays.
Best next step Use a traceable method and keep the proof.

Operational payment guidance 03

Payment receiver email

Where payment proof should be linked to your enquiry.

The payment receiver email should be used exactly as provided in writing and should match the details used for your enquiry whenever possible.

If payment is being sent from a different name or email address, say so clearly when you send proof so the transfer can be matched correctly.

Why it matters Matching details speeds up review.
Best next step Send proof with your full name and enquiry reference.

Operational payment guidance 04

Proof-of-payment instruction

What proof should include after payment is sent.

Send a clear screenshot, receipt, or confirmation record showing the amount, date, sender, and any payment reference that helps identify the transfer.

If the proof is incomplete or unclear, confirmation may take longer because the team may need to request more information before scheduling can proceed.

Why it matters Clear proof allows faster confirmation.
Best next step Send proof as soon as payment is completed.

Operational payment guidance 05

WhatsApp assistance

When WhatsApp is useful during payment follow-up.

WhatsApp is useful for quick payment follow-up, sending proof, and confirming whether a transfer has been received for review.

For longer context or full case details, use the enquiry form or email as well. WhatsApp works best for fast follow-up, not for replacing a full written summary.

Why it matters The right channel makes follow-up easier.
Best next step Use WhatsApp for quick proof and short follow-up.

Operational payment guidance 06

Manual confirmation workflow

What happens between payment and a confirmed booking.

After proof is received, the payment is reviewed, matched to the enquiry, and checked against the requested timing.

If everything is in order, written confirmation follows. If something is missing, the team will reply with the next required step before treating the appointment as confirmed.

Why it matters Manual review protects clients and scheduling.
Best next step Wait for written confirmation before treating the appointment as booked.

Operational payment guidance 07

36-hour scheduling rule

How the minimum timing rule affects bookings.

Appointments must allow enough time for payment verification, manual review, and proper preparation. Times inside the 36-hour window are not treated as automatically available.

If your timing is tight, contact the team before paying so the most realistic option can be discussed first.

Why it matters Rushed bookings create avoidable problems.
Best next step Check timing before payment if the matter is urgent.

Expanded context 01

What the rule changes in practice

How payment rules protect clarity on both sides.

Payment rules are there to protect clarity, not to make the process harder. When methods, proof, timing, and confirmation are handled in the right order, clients know exactly where they stand.

That reduces confusion, prevents rushed assumptions, and makes follow-up safer for everyone involved.

Why it matters Clear rules reduce avoidable confusion.
Best next step Follow the payment steps in order.

Expanded context 02

What to do first

Start with the method, timing, and correct payment details.

Before sending funds, confirm the correct payment route and make sure the scheduling window is realistic. If you need bank transfer or crypto details, request them in writing first.

Once payment is sent, the next immediate step is to send proof clearly so the transfer can be matched without delay.

Why it matters Correct setup prevents later follow-up.
Best next step Confirm details first, then pay.

Expanded context 03

What to avoid

Avoid payment mistakes that slow down confirmation.

Do not send funds to unconfirmed details, rely on outdated wallet information, assume payment automatically reserves a consultation time, or wait too long to send proof.

These are the common issues that create back-and-forth, especially when a client is paying internationally or using a different name or account.

Why it matters Avoidable errors can delay approval of the booking.
Best next step Use only current written payment details.

Expanded context 04

How follow-up works

What happens after proof reaches the team.

After proof is received, the team checks whether the transfer is clear, traceable, and correctly linked to the enquiry. If something is missing, you may be asked for a better screenshot, a transaction reference, or a short clarification.

If everything is clear, written confirmation follows. That confirmation is what matters for scheduling, not the transfer alone.

Why it matters Clear follow-up prevents uncertainty.
Best next step Wait for written confirmation before planning around the time.

Expanded context 05

Why the boundary matters

Why payment alone is not treated as a booked appointment.

Treating payment and booking as separate steps protects clients from assumptions and protects the schedule from conflicts or incomplete information.

It is a professional boundary, not a formality. It keeps the process safer, clearer, and easier to verify.

Why it matters Clear boundaries protect trust.
Best next step Do not treat payment alone as confirmation.

Expanded context 06

If you need a different payment route

Alternative payment arrangements should always be confirmed first.

If PIX, PayPal, Payoneer, or Wise are not practical in your situation, contact the team before paying. Direct bank transfer, Bitcoin, or USDT may be possible, but only on the basis of current written details.

That extra check matters because different payment routes may require specific instructions, confirmation thresholds, or identification details.

Why it matters Alternative routes need written confirmation first.
Best next step Ask before using a different payment route.

Expanded context 07

Urgent timing and scheduling

Urgent timing should be checked before payment is sent.

If your question is urgent, do not assume an immediate slot is available just because payment can be made quickly. Timing still depends on review, availability, and the 36-hour rule.

The safest route is to contact the team first, explain the urgency clearly, and confirm what timing is realistic before you pay.

Why it matters Urgent matters still need realistic scheduling.
Best next step Ask about timing before paying if the matter is urgent.

What to avoid

When to contact before paying instead of guessing.

Contact the team before paying if you are unsure which method to use, if you need crypto or bank transfer details, if the payment will come from a different name, or if the timing is especially tight.

That short check can prevent the most common payment problems and makes the confirmation process much smoother.

Why it matters Short clarification can prevent bigger problems.
