Pricing model

Pricing model

The pricing structure applied by Immigrate to Brazil is stage-based and scope-defined.

Strategic consultation, intake assessment, and route evaluation are independent professional services. These stages involve structured analysis, procedural planning, and risk evaluation, and are billed accordingly.

Execution-stage support, including document preparation, filing coordination, authority-facing communication, and procedural follow-through, constitutes a separate scope of work. It is not included within consultation unless explicitly defined in writing.

Payment for an earlier stage does not imply inclusion of later stages. Each stage must be separately defined, accepted, and, where applicable, formalized.

Where legal representation is involved, fees related to legal services are governed by the specific contract between the client and the lawyer. That contract prevails over general payment structures described here.

Section 02

Accepted payment methods

Payment may be accepted through the following methods, depending on the nature of the service and jurisdictional context:

  • PIX
  • PayPal
  • Payoneer
  • Wise
  • Direct bank transfer
  • Bitcoin (BTC)
  • USDT (Tether)

Not all methods are appropriate for all transactions. Payment instructions must always be obtained from current, written communication specific to the active matter.

Clients must not rely on:

  • previously used payment details
  • forwarded account information
  • screenshots or informal references

Only the most recent written instructions issued for the specific service engagement should be used.

Section 03

Billing structure

Payment is generally required in advance of service delivery.

This includes:

  • consultation and strategy sessions (paid prior to booking confirmation)
  • execution-stage services (paid prior to initiation of work for the defined scope)

Where a matter progresses from consultation to execution support, the commercial structure for the execution phase must be agreed separately in writing.

This staged billing model ensures clarity of responsibility and prevents overlap between advisory and execution obligations.

Where legal services are engaged, billing for those services follows the terms defined in the lawyer-client agreement, which governs that relationship.

Section 04

Scope versus payment

Payment applies strictly to the scope defined for the accepted service stage.

It does not create:

  • open-ended advisory access
  • ongoing support beyond the defined stage
  • responsibility for future or undefined work
  • inclusion of services not explicitly agreed

Each stage operates independently unless formally extended.

This structure protects both parties by ensuring that expectations remain aligned with what has been expressly agreed, rather than assumed.

Where a separate contract exists, particularly with a lawyer, that contract governs the scope of work and corresponding payment obligations for legal services.

Section 05

Verification, booking control, and scheduling discipline

All payments are subject to verification before a booking or service stage is confirmed.

A transfer alone does not constitute confirmation. Confirmation requires:

  • verification of receipt
  • matching of payment to the correct matter
  • written confirmation issued by Immigrate to Brazil

Appointments and consultations operate under a structured scheduling framework.

As a general rule, confirmed sessions are scheduled with a minimum preparation and control window of 36 hours after confirmation. This ensures adequate time for:

  • intake review
  • preparation
  • quality control of the advisory stage

Exceptions to the 36-hour rule

Exceptions may be considered in specific circumstances, including urgent or time-sensitive matters. However:

  • exceptions are not automatic
  • availability must be confirmed in advance
  • prior contact is required before any assumption of expedited scheduling

No client should rely on an accelerated timeline without explicit written confirmation.

Section 06

Security and payment integrity

All payments must be made using verified and current instructions issued directly for the active engagement.

Clients should not:

  • reuse outdated payment details
  • rely on third-party forwarding of payment instructions
  • proceed without confirming accuracy where uncertainty exists

This is a preventative control. Financial misdirection is significantly more difficult to correct after a transfer has been executed.

Immigrate to Brazil is not responsible for losses resulting from payments sent to incorrect or outdated details not issued in current written instructions.

Section 07

Non-payment consequences

Where payment for a defined stage is not completed, cannot be verified, or is reversed, the corresponding service stage may:

  • not be initiated
  • be suspended
  • be postponed
  • be cancelled

Service continuity depends on confirmed and compliant payment at each stage.

No obligation exists to proceed with consultation, execution support, or coordination activities until payment requirements for that stage have been satisfied.

Financial flexibility and case-based consideration

In certain circumstances, Immigrate to Brazil may offer:

  • adjusted pricing structures
  • staged payment arrangements
  • reduced fees based on demonstrated need

This may apply particularly in cases involving individuals from underrepresented or vulnerable groups, including but not limited to:

  • LGBTQ+ individuals
  • Indigenous persons
  • migrants in vulnerable conditions
  • individuals facing structural or legal barriers

If financial difficulty is a factor, it should be clearly stated at the time of initial contact or before payment is made. Requests are assessed case by case and are not automatically granted.

Public legal aid alternatives

Where a client's income falls within applicable thresholds, they may be eligible for free legal assistance through the Defensoria Publica (Public Defender's Office) in Brazil.

Clients are encouraged to consider this option where appropriate. Immigrate to Brazil does not replace public legal aid structures and does not discourage their use.

Section 09

Relationship with legal contracts and governing terms

Where a client enters into a formal agreement with a qualified lawyer, that contract governs:

  • the scope of legal services
  • the applicable fee structure
  • responsibility and liability for legal work

In the event of any inconsistency between these Payment Terms and a lawyer-client agreement, the lawyer-client agreement prevails with respect to legal services.

These Payment Terms apply to advisory and coordination services provided by Immigrate to Brazil and operate alongside, but do not override, formal legal contracts.
