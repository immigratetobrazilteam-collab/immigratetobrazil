LGPD compliance statement

This page explains how Monique Fernandes approaches data handling under the LGPD framework.

It is designed to help users understand the Brazilian privacy structure that applies to contact, intake, service delivery, and record handling within the practice.

Section 02

Identification of the controlador

The practice responsible for the handling of data should be clearly identifiable.

For the purposes described on this site, Monique Fernandes / Immigrate to Brazil is the relevant contact point for understanding how data is handled and how requests should be made.

Section 03

Data processing purpose

Data should be used for identifiable service and operational reasons.

This includes handling inquiries, evaluating immigration matters, scheduling consultation, communicating with users, managing documents, verifying payments, delivering services, and maintaining legal and administrative records.

Section 04

Legal bases under Brazilian law

The legal basis should follow the activity involved.

Depending on the context, processing may rely on consent, contract-related steps, legal or regulatory obligations, legitimate interests, protection of rights, or other bases recognized by Brazilian law.

Section 05

Brazilian user rights

The LGPD gives data subjects rights that should be understandable and usable.

Depending on the case, a person may request confirmation, access, correction, anonymization or deletion where legally available, portability where applicable, information about sharing, or other rights recognized by the LGPD.

Section 06

Data retention and sharing

Records should be kept and shared only as justified by the service and the law.

Monique may retain and share data where necessary for the service stage, compliance, communication, rights defense, or other lawful operational needs, always with attention to relevance and proportionality.

Section 07

ANPD reference

Users should know that ANPD exists as part of the Brazilian privacy framework.

The ANPD is the Brazilian authority connected to the LGPD framework. A user may contact the practice first for clarification, while still recognizing that public authority routes also exist.

Section 08

Contact for requests

Requests should be sent through the official contact channel.

LGPD-related questions and requests may be sent to moniquefadv@gmail.com, with enough detail to identify the person, the request, and the issue involved.
